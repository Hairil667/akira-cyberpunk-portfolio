## ⚡ Key Features

- **3D Procedural Aerodynamic Hypercar Model**: Detailed carbon fiber monocoque chassis, aerodynamic front hood & splitter, sloping cockpit roofline, active rear spoiler, side air scoops, rear diffuser with dual exhausts, and alloy wheels with gold brake calipers.
- **Bespoke Paint Customizer**: Real-time car paint shader update:
  - `Obsidian Black`
  - `Liquid Titanium Silver`
  - `Rosso Corsa Pearl Red`
  - `Monaco Gold`
  - `Cyber Electric Blue`
  - `British Racing Green`
- **Interactive Studio Actions (100% Mobile & Touch Compatible)**:
  - `[REV ENGINE 🔊]`: V8 Twin-Turbo engine rumble & turbo whistle synthesized with Web Audio API.
  - `[HEADLIGHTS 💡]`: Toggle LED Matrix headlights ON/OFF.
  - `[ACTIVE WING 🚀]`: Raise/lower rear active aero wing.
  - `[SPIN WHEELS 🏎️]`: Spin 4 alloy wheels in real-time.
  - `[STUDIO CAMERAS]`: Instant camera angle presets (Front 3/4, Profile, Rear, Studio Reset).
- **100% Mobile Responsive**: Seamless 1-finger orbit drag, pinch zoom, and responsive touch dock.
- **Performance Telemetry & Engineering Showcase**: 0-100 km/h (1.85s), Top Speed (450 km/h), 1,600 HP output.
