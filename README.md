## ⛩️ Features

- **3D Sakura Storm Background**: Over 280+ procedural 3D cherry blossom petals with realistic wind turbulence, drifting physics, floating spirit lanterns, and background Torii gate silhouette.
- **3D Samurai Kabuto & Oni Mempo War Mask**: 360° rotatable 3D samurai helmet with golden crescent moon horn (*Maedate*), riveted helmet plates (*Hachi*), layered neck guard (*Shikoro*), glowing demon eyes, fangs, and dual crossed katanas.
- **Interactive Stances**:
  - `[IAIJUTSU]` (Quick-draw blade stance & cyan aura)
  - `[BLOOD OATH]` (Crimson Oni flame rage)
  - `[GOLDEN DAWN]` (Divine golden armor glow)
- **3D Katana Forge (The Dojo)**:
  - **妖刀村正 (Murasama Demon Blade)**: Folded crimson tamahagane steel with high-frequency plasma hamon.
  - **雷切 (Raikiri Lightning Blade)**: Cyan titanium blade crackling with electric coils.
  - **草薙剣 (Kusanagi Sacred Blade)**: Divine golden blade with sacred aura.
  - Controls: 360° orbit, mouse wheel zoom, Katana Slash animation, and Wireframe mode.
- **Interactive Katana Slash Blade Trail**: Slicing the cursor or finger across the screen generates a glowing blade cut trail with flying sparks and synthesized katana unsheathing sounds.
- **Scroll of Bushido (武士道七徳)**: 7 virtues of software craftsmanship (Rectitude, Courage, Benevolence, Respect, Honesty, Honor, Loyalty).
- **Procedural Web Audio API Synthesizer**: Zero-dependency audio engine synthesizing katana cuts, blade clashing rings, taiko drum impacts, and koto pentatonic notes.
